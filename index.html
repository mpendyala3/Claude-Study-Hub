Claude Study Hub

Four Claude certifications, one place to prepare

Pick an exam below. Each track is self-contained and built the same way: an Overview with the published blueprint, Docs covering every objective, Exercises graded in the browser, and a timed Mock exam written harder than the real one. No accounts, no network calls — everything runs locally in this page.

The order they are meant to be taken in CCAO-F is the entry point and assumes nothing. CCDV-F and CCAR-F sit at the same level but point in different directions — Developer is how do I build it, Architect Foundations is which design is correct and why, and the two overlap heavily on tools, MCP and context. CCAR-P is the only one that genuinely builds on another: it adds RAG, evaluation, governance, regulated deployment and stakeholder work on top of the Foundations architecture material, and its items assume you already hold that.

CCAR-P Architect · Professional

Claude Certified Architect — Professional

The senior exam: end-to-end solution design, RAG and retrieval strategy, integration protocol selection, evaluation and observability, governance and regulated deployment, and the stakeholder and lifecycle work an architect owns. Seven domains, thirty-eight objectives. CCAR-F is not a prerequisite — but it is the gentler ramp, and its mechanisms carry straight into this paper.

How to work a track Read the Docs, then do every Exercise, then sit the Mock exam in Exam mode — 120 minutes, no feedback until you submit. When you get a question wrong, its domain tag is the exact section of the Docs to go back to. All four mocks are deliberately harder than the live papers, so aim for 850+ before you book.
